Step-by-Step Triage

Immediate First-Aid Guidelines

Take these immediate stabilizing actions while en route to our emergency pavilion.

Scenario 01 • Ingestion 01

Suspected Poison / Toxin Ingestion

  • ✦ DO NOT induce vomiting unless explicitly instructed by a veterinary toxicologist (caustic chemicals or petroleum products cause esophageal chemical burns upon regurgitation).
  • ✦ Secure the Substance: Safely retrieve packaging, plant leaves, or medication bottles for chemical composition analysis.
  • ✦ Note Time & Quantity: Record precise timestamp of ingestion to calculate therapeutic antidote windows.
Scenario 02 • Hyperthermia 02

Heatstroke & Thermal Crisis

  • ✦ Symptoms: Heavy panting, dark red/brick gums, thick saliva, weakness, or stumbling.
  • ✦ Active Cooling Protocol: Apply tepid (cool, not freezing) water to paw pads, groin, and neck. Place near gentle air circulation.
  • ✦ Never Use Ice Water: Extreme cold causes peripheral vasoconstriction, trapping lethal heat within internal organs.
Scenario 03 • Airway 03

Choking & Airway Obstruction

  • ✦ Gentle Visual Sweep: Carefully open the jaw and inspect the pharynx. Only remove a visible, loose foreign object if it does not require force.
  • ✦ Modified Heimlich: For dogs, place hands below the ribcage and administer firm upward abdominal thrusts while standing behind the animal.
Scenario 04 • Bleeding 04

Acute Hemorrhage & Trauma

  • ✦ Direct Continuous Pressure: Place sterile gauze or clean linen directly over the wound and maintain firm, uninterrupted pressure for 5–7 minutes.
  • ✦ Prevent Movement: Keep the companion lying down and wrapped in warm fleece to counter systemic shock.
