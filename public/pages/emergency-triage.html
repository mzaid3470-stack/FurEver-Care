Emergency & Triage

Know the warning signs, respond calmly, and get professional veterinary help when an urgent situation occurs.

Urgent Warning Signs

Difficulty breathing, collapse, uncontrolled bleeding, seizures, severe trauma, suspected poisoning, or sudden inability to stand can require immediate veterinary attention.

First Response

Stay calm, keep the pet as still and comfortable as possible, and remove immediate hazards. Avoid giving food, medication, or home remedies unless a veterinary professional advises it.

Getting Veterinary Care

Contact an emergency veterinary service as soon as possible. When calling, explain what happened, when it started, the pet's symptoms, and any known exposure or injury.

Everyday checklist

  • Call a veterinarian for serious or rapidly worsening symptoms
  • Keep emergency contact details accessible
  • Transport injured pets carefully
  • Take packaging or relevant information if poisoning is suspected

Care with confidence

Small, consistent choices can make everyday pet care safer, calmer, and more enjoyable.
