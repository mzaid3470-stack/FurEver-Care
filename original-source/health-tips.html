Preventive Clinical Science

Health & Longevity Masterclass

Translating veterinary medicine into actionable domestic rituals. Proactive preventive health extends companion longevity by an average of 2.4 years.

Discipline 01 • Dental Health N°01

Oral Care & Periodontal Prevention

By age three, over 80% of dogs and 70% of cats exhibit early stage periodontal inflammation. Unchecked plaque bacteria enter the bloodstream, compromising renal and cardiac valve tissue.

Daily Protocol: Enzymatic poultry-flavored toothpaste with soft silicone finger brushes. Never use human toothpaste containing fluoride or foaming agents.

Discipline 02 • Metabolism N°02

Metabolic Weight & Lean Body Condition

Maintaining an ideal Body Condition Score (BCS 4-5 on a 9-point scale) alleviates stress on joint cartilage, stabilizes insulin sensitivity, and preserves cardiovascular endurance.

The Rib Palpation Test: Ribs should be easily felt without excess pressure, with an observable abdominal tuck when viewed from above.

Immunology Protocol

Core & Non-Core Vaccination Schedule

Based on American Animal Hospital Association (AAHA) and WSAVA international guidelines.

Canine Core Vaccines

  • ✦ DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvo, Parainfluenza): 8, 12, 16 weeks; booster at 1 year, then triennially.
  • ✦ Rabies: 16 weeks; 1-year booster, followed by 3-year immunization cycles.
  • ✦ Bordetella & Leptospirosis: Annually for social or outdoor explorer dogs.

Feline Core Vaccines

  • ✦ FVRCP (Rhinotracheitis, Calicivirus, Panleukopenia): 8, 12, 16 weeks; 1-year booster, then every 3 years.
  • ✦ FeLV (Feline Leukemia): Highly recommended for kittens and all outdoor-accessible cats.
  • ✦ Rabies: Non-adjuvanted formulation administered according to regional statutes.
N°03

Early Symptom Detection

Subtle behavioral shifts—such as decreased jumping, excessive drinking (polydipsia), or litter box hesitance—are the earliest indicators of internal organ adaptation.

N°04

Geriatric Companion Care

Senior pets (7+ years) benefit from orthopedic memory bedding, glucosamine/chondroitin supplementation, and bi-annual blood chemistry panels.

N°05

Mental Stress & Adaptogens

Environmental tranquility reduces cortisol levels. Organic Ashwagandha, L-Theanine, and synthetic feline pheromone diffusers foster emotional serenity.
