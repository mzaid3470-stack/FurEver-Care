Aesthetic Rituals

Grooming & Coat Care

Elevating grooming beyond maintenance into a sensory bonding ritual. Explore clinical hygiene techniques, botanical coat conditioning, and gentle handling methodologies.

Ritual 01 • Daily Coat Care N°01

Botanical Brushing & Undercoat De-Shedding

Brushing stimulates cutaneous micro-circulation and distributes natural sebum across the hair shaft. For double-coated breeds, use an undercoat rake followed by a natural boar-bristle brush in layered sweeps.

  • ✦ Short Coats: Rubber curry mitt 3x weekly to capture loose keratin.
  • ✦ Silky / Long Coats: Daily pin brush with leave-in botanical rosewater spray.
  • ✦ Double Coats: Line-brushing technique moving from caudal to cranial.

Ritual 02 • Hydrotherapy N°02

Botanical Bathing & Thermal Balance

Water temperature must strictly align with canine and feline skin tolerance (37°C / 98.6°F). Never apply concentrated shampoo directly to dry coats—pre-dilute with warm water to prevent follicle irritation.

Sanctuary Tip: Always place a non-slip rubber mat in the basin. Ensuring secure footing reduces companion bath anxiety by over 80%.
N°03

Nail Trimming & Quick Safety

Identify the pink vascular quick inside the claw matrix. Trim at a 45-degree angle with precision stainless steel shears. Keep styptic powder within arm's reach.

Recommended cadence: Every 14–21 days to prevent postural hyperextension.
N°04

Auditory Canal & Ear Hygiene

Never insert cotton swabs into the L-shaped ear canal. Use certified non-alcoholic drying flushes, gently massage the ear base for 30 seconds, and wipe with sterile gauze.

Crucial for floppy-eared breeds (Spaniels, Bassets, Lops) post-swimming.
N°05

Seasonal Coat Transitions

Spring and Autumn trigger photoperiod-induced molting. Incorporate dietary wild Alaskan salmon oil and humidity misting to preserve the epidermal moisture barrier.

Combines internal omega supplementation with external hydration.
