Sanctuary Chronicles

Lives Transformed Through Love

Every adoption is a solemn promise. Read how our sanctuary alumni discovered security, companionship, and boundless happiness with their dedicated guardians.

Chronicle 01 • Adopted April 2026 N°01

Rosie Parker’s Journey to Peace

“She was so timid when she first walked into our parlor. Today, Rosie Parker curls at my feet by the fireplace like she has belonged here for lifetimes.”

Found malnourished and fearful, Rosie Parker spent five months in our botanical stress therapy rehabilitation before matching with the Montgomery family. Today, she enjoys daily morning strolls through rose gardens and gourmet venison pâté.

Guardian: Victoria Montgomery • Upper East Haven Alumni
Chronicle 02 • Adopted January 2026 N°02

The Duo: Daisy & Lily

“Adopting a bonded pair was the single greatest decision of our lives. Watching them groom each other in the morning sun brings pure tranquility.”

These two sisters refused to be separated. FurEver Care kept them together through complete health diagnostics and foster care until Julian & Claire provided a sunlit sanctuary home complete with custom teakwood climbing towers.

Guardians: Julian & Claire Thorne • Metropolitan Atelier Alumni
Chronicle 03 • Adopted June 2026 N°03

Max Bennett’s Joy Binkies

“Max showed us how much personality a free-roaming rabbit possesses. He leaps with sheer exuberance across our living room rugs!”

Surrendered due to allergies in his prior household, Max found an idyllic free-roaming environment with Dr. Daniel Brooks's family, enjoying organic dandelion harvests and willow puzzles daily.

Guardian: Marcus & Evelyn Sterling • Highland Valley Alumni
