Behavioral Philosophy

Mind, Psychology & Training

Unlocking communication through mutual trust, clear marker signals, and positive reinforcement science rather than intimidation.

Methodology 01 • Marker Signals N°01

Positive Reinforcement & Clicker Precision

Animals learn by associating instantaneous markers with primary rewards. A distinct click sound or verbal marker ("Yes!") bridges the split-second gap between desirable behavior and high-value treats.

Sound Laboratory: Clicker Marker
Frequency: 2.8 kHz • Calibrated Attention Signal

Discipline 02 • Core Signals

Foundational Commands: Sit, Stay & Recall

  • ✦ Emergency Recall ("Come"): The single most vital life-saving command. Never call a dog to reprimand them.
  • ✦ Impulse Control ("Wait / Leave It"): Reinforces self-regulation before doorways, food bowls, and street crossings.
  • ✦ Loose-Leash Heel: Reward eye contact every 3–5 paces to make walking beside you the most stimulating choice.
Discipline 03 • Domestic Harmony

Potty Training & Enzymatic Hygiene

  • ✦ 2-Hour Interval Rule: Take puppies outside immediately after waking, playing, and 15 minutes after meals.
  • ✦ Designated Elimination Sanctuary: Consistently lead to the exact same quiet outdoor grass perimeter.
  • ✦ Enzymatic Cleaners Only: Break down uric acid crystals completely to extinguish instinctive scent markers.
Discipline 04 • Early Windows

Socialization & Stimulus Neutrality

The critical canine socialization period occurs between weeks 3 and 14 (weeks 2–9 for kittens). The goal is calm neutrality—not mandatory interaction—when exposed to novel textures, umbrella sounds, and passing bicycles.

Discipline 05 • Emotional Resilience

Separation Anxiety & De-sensitization

Desensitize departure cues (picking up keys, putting on coats) without actually leaving. Provide long-lasting frozen lick mats filled with salmon pâté to trigger natural dopamine release.
