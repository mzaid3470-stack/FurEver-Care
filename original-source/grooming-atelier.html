Grooming Studio

Gentle grooming routines that keep coats, skin, nails, ears, and everyday hygiene in good condition.

Coat Care

Regular brushing removes loose hair, helps prevent mats, and gives you an opportunity to notice changes in the skin or coat early.

Nails, Ears & Hygiene

Nails should be trimmed carefully when needed, while ears should be checked routinely. Use only pet-appropriate cleaning products and avoid inserting objects into the ear canal.

A Calm Routine

Introduce grooming gradually with treats and praise. If a pet becomes highly distressed, stop and seek help from a professional groomer or veterinarian.

Everyday checklist

  • Use tools suited to the coat type
  • Check skin for lumps, redness, or irritation
  • Trim nails carefully and regularly
  • Make grooming calm, gradual, and rewarding

Care with confidence

Small, consistent choices can make everyday pet care safer, calmer, and more enjoyable.
